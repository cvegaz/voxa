This template records the results of professional soccer matches. Each row represents a match played and includes the date of the match, the home team and the away team, the goals scored by each, the stadium where it was played, and the competition it belongs to (league, cup, or international tournament). The data is captured by narrating the result of the match out loud; for example: "On June 15, 2026, Real Madrid hosted Barcelona and won three to one at the Santiago Bernabeu in La Liga." The system must extract the date in YYYY-MM-DD format, the full names of both teams, the goals as whole numbers, the name of the stadium, and the corresponding competition.
